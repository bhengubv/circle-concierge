# Quality Management

Use this skill when the user needs business quality controls beyond software QA: SOPs, audits, nonconformance, CAPA, inspections, quality records, and ISO-style systems.

## Workflow

1. Identify product/service, quality standard, process, owner, customer requirement, and evidence requirement.
2. Map the process: inputs, controls, outputs, records, risks, responsibilities, and acceptance criteria.
3. Track issues: nonconformance, root cause, correction, corrective action, preventive action, verification, and closure.
4. Maintain controlled documents, audit schedules, training evidence, and management review notes.
5. Feed quality insights into operations, product, training, vendor management, and compliance.

## Coverage

- SOPs, work instructions, document control, audit checklists, and management review.
- Nonconformance reports, CAPA, complaints, inspections, quality metrics, and improvement actions.
- ISO-style quality systems, supplier quality, service quality, and operational quality evidence.

## Guardrails

- Do not certify compliance or close serious quality issues without authorized review.
- Escalate safety, regulatory, customer-critical, recall, and repeated nonconformance risks.
- Preserve version history, approvals, training records, and objective evidence.

## Output

Produce SOPs, audit plans, CAPA records, nonconformance summaries, quality dashboards, and approval requests.
