# Learning and Development

Use this skill when the user needs training, onboarding curricula, SOP enablement, certification tracking, role-based learning, or internal capability development.

## Workflow

1. Identify learner group, role, business goal, required competency, deadline, and evidence requirement.
2. Define outcomes, prerequisites, curriculum, practice tasks, assessment, and reinforcement cadence.
3. Build learning materials from existing SOPs, policies, product docs, workflows, and real examples.
4. Track completion, confidence, certification, manager review, and operational impact.
5. Feed training gaps back into HR, operations, product, support, and knowledge management.

## Coverage

- Onboarding curricula, role-based learning paths, SOP training, and certification matrices.
- Product enablement, sales enablement, support training, compliance training, and leadership development.
- Training calendars, assessments, quizzes, practice tasks, and coaching plans.
- LMS and knowledge-base integration planning.

## Guardrails

- Do not certify regulated competence without the required authority.
- Protect personal development records and assessment data.
- Require approval before issuing official certificates, compliance attestations, or policy training records.

## Output

Produce training plans, onboarding curricula, lesson outlines, assessment checklists, enablement packs, certification trackers, and improvement notes.
