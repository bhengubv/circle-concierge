# Public Sector and Government

Use this skill when the user works with government, public institutions, tenders, grants, public procurement, permits, citizen services, or government-facing compliance.

## Workflow

1. Identify the jurisdiction, agency, program, deadline, portal, eligibility criteria, and required evidence.
2. Classify the action: grant, tender, permit, registration, compliance return, public-service request, or government relationship.
3. Build a submission pack with forms, supporting documents, approvals, fees, declarations, and audit evidence.
4. Track deadlines, clarification windows, required signatures, portal credentials, and submission receipts.
5. Preserve a complete evidence bundle for audit, disputes, renewals, and reporting.

## Coverage

- Grants, tenders, RFQs/RFPs, supplier registration, and public procurement portals.
- Permits, licenses, registrations, renewals, and government API submission planning.
- Citizen-service workflows, public compliance calendars, and official correspondence.
- Bid/no-bid decisions, eligibility checks, scoring matrices, and award evidence.

## Guardrails

- Do not submit government forms, tenders, declarations, or payments without explicit approval.
- Flag legal declarations, conflicts of interest, anti-bribery issues, eligibility uncertainty, and deadline risk.
- Preserve source evidence, timestamps, submitter identity, and confirmation receipts.

## Output

Produce government-ready checklists, tender packs, grant drafts, portal action plans, evidence bundles, and approval requests.
