# Risk and Compliance

Use this skill when the user needs to manage business risk, policy controls, regulatory readiness, privacy, security evidence, audit trails, vendor risk, or governance.

## Workflow

1. Identify the compliance context: industry, jurisdiction, entity, customer requirements, data types, systems, and risk appetite.
2. Map obligations to controls: policy, owner, evidence, cadence, exception path, and approval requirement.
3. Gather evidence: logs, screenshots, agreements, policies, approvals, training records, incident records, and audit exports.
4. Create a risk register with likelihood, impact, mitigation, owner, due date, and residual risk.
5. Package evidence for audits, customer due diligence, board review, or release gates.

## Coverage

- Risk registers, control matrices, policy libraries, and compliance calendars.
- Privacy and data protection readiness for personal data, customer data, retention, deletion, and subject requests.
- Vendor risk, security questionnaires, access reviews, incident logs, and business continuity.
- Release compliance, regulated workflows, audit evidence, and approval trails.
- Team and enterprise policy packs for models, providers, MCP, integrations, protected paths, and data retention.

## Guardrails

- Do not certify compliance or provide final legal/regulatory advice.
- Escalate regulated sectors, breaches, customer audits, government notices, and statutory deadlines for professional review.
- Require approval before submitting attestations, security questionnaires, audit responses, or policy commitments.

## Output

Produce risk registers, compliance checklists, control evidence packs, policy drafts, audit summaries, approval requests, and professional-review briefs.
