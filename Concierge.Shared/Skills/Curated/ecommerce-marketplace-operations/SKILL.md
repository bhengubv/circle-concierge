# Ecommerce and Marketplace Operations

Use this skill when the user runs an online store, marketplace channel, DTC brand, reseller operation, dropship workflow, or multi-channel ecommerce business.

## Workflow

1. Identify channels, products, catalog structure, inventory source, fulfillment model, payment flow, returns policy, and marketplace rules.
2. Map the order lifecycle: listing, pricing, promotion, checkout, payment, fraud checks, picking, packing, shipping, delivery, returns, refunds, and support.
3. Track catalog evidence: SKUs, images, descriptions, variants, dimensions, compliance, taxes, stock, and channel-specific requirements.
4. Monitor performance: conversion, traffic, cart abandonment, AOV, return rate, margin, stockouts, reviews, ads, and channel health.
5. Gate high-impact actions: price changes, refunds, promotions, marketplace submissions, payment settings, and bulk catalog edits.

## Coverage

- Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Takealot-style channels, AliExpress/Taobao sourcing, social commerce, and custom storefronts.
- Catalog ops, listings, pricing, inventory, orders, fulfillment, returns, refunds, reviews, customer messages, marketplace policy, and seller health.
- Payments, tax readiness, fraud risk, shipping, logistics, ads, analytics, merchandising, and channel expansion.

## Guardrails

- Do not publish listings, change prices, issue refunds, place supplier orders, or alter payment settings without approval.
- Flag counterfeit/IP risk, unsafe products, restricted goods, tax/customs issues, fraud, policy violations, and misleading claims.
- Preserve listing versions, order evidence, customer communications, refund approvals, and marketplace notices.

## Output

Produce ecommerce operating plans, catalog checklists, listing drafts, order/returns workflows, channel scorecards, promotion briefs, and approval requests.
