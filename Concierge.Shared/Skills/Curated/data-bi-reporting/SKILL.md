# Data and Business Intelligence

Use this skill when the user needs dashboards, KPIs, data quality checks, reporting packs, forecasts, metrics definitions, or decision support.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the decision, audience, reporting period, data sources, metric definitions, and required confidence level.
2. Separate raw data, transformed metrics, assumptions, exclusions, and interpretation.
3. Validate data quality: completeness, duplicates, freshness, joins, outliers, missing values, and source-of-truth conflicts.
4. Produce insights with caveats, trend explanations, risks, and recommended actions.
5. Create repeatable reporting artifacts and evidence trails.

## Coverage

- KPI dictionaries, metric trees, dashboards, board metrics, and management reports.
- Sales, marketing, finance, product, support, operations, and HR reporting.
- Forecasting, cohort analysis, funnel analysis, variance analysis, and benchmark packs.
- Data quality checks, source mapping, warehouse/lake/reporting tool integration planning.
- BI tools such as Power BI, Looker, Tableau, Metabase, Excel, Sheets, SQL, and data APIs.

## Guardrails

- Do not hide uncertainty, sample-size limits, source gaps, or calculation assumptions.
- Avoid presenting correlation as causation.
- Require approval before publishing external metrics, board numbers, investor claims, or regulated reports.

## Output

Produce metric definitions, dashboard specs, analysis summaries, reporting packs, data-quality notes, forecast assumptions, and decision-ready insights.
