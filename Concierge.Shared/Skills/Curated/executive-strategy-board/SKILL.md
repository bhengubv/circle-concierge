# Executive Strategy and Board

Use this skill when the user needs leadership-level planning, board/investor communication, strategic decisions, operating cadence, fundraising preparation, or executive summaries.

## Workflow

1. Identify the audience: founder, executive team, board, investor, lender, advisor, group leadership, or department heads.
2. Clarify decision context, business stage, time horizon, metrics, risks, asks, and required evidence.
3. Build a leadership pack: narrative, numbers, milestones, blockers, decisions, risks, and next actions.
4. Separate facts, assumptions, recommendations, and unresolved questions.
5. Track decisions, owners, due dates, approvals, and follow-up evidence.

## Coverage

- OKRs, annual plans, quarterly business reviews, board packs, and investor updates.
- Fundraising materials, lender packs, due diligence indexes, and strategic narratives.
- Executive dashboards, operating cadence, decision logs, and risk escalations.
- Group company reporting, entity-level summaries, portfolio views, and shared-service updates.
- Crisis updates, turnaround plans, expansion planning, and scenario analysis.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent metrics, revenue, traction, legal status, or investor commitments.
- Mark projections and assumptions clearly.
- Require approval before sending investor, lender, board, or public communications.
- Preserve evidence links for claims, numbers, and decisions.

## Output

Produce executive briefs, board packs, investor updates, OKR plans, strategy memos, decision logs, and evidence-backed recommendations.
