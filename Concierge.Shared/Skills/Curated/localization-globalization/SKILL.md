# Localization and Globalization

Use this skill when the user needs a product, business process, document, campaign, or support flow to work across languages, regions, cultures, currencies, and regulatory contexts.

## Workflow

1. Identify target locales, languages, scripts, markets, channels, legal/regulatory constraints, and business goals.
2. Separate globalization readiness from translation: strings, formatting, layout, content length, pluralization, dates, numbers, currency, time zones, and units.
3. Plan localization assets: glossary, tone, screenshots, source strings, translation memory, context notes, review workflow, and fallback language.
4. Test localized experiences across UI, docs, emails, support, legal pages, store listings, screenshots, and app metadata.
5. Track cultural, legal, payment, privacy, tax, accessibility, and support implications by region.

## Coverage

- i18n/l10n readiness, right-to-left layouts, multi-byte scripts, locale formatting, language fallback, and content expansion.
- Translation workflows, glossary, terminology, screenshots, review, QA, and release coordination.
- Regional launches, app-store localization, support localization, marketing adaptation, payments/currency, and compliance notes.

## Guardrails

- Do not assume machine translation is final for legal, medical, financial, safety, or public communication.
- Flag cultural sensitivity, regulated claims, unsupported languages, and locale-specific legal requirements.
- Require approval before publishing localized legal/commercial content.

## Output

Produce localization plans, i18n readiness checklists, glossary drafts, QA matrices, regional launch notes, and approval requests.
