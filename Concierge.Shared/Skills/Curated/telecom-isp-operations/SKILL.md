# Telecom and ISP Operations

Use this skill when the user operates or supports telecom, ISP, connectivity, managed network, wireless, fiber, VoIP, or communications-service workflows.

## Workflow

1. Identify service type, customer/site, network segment, SLA, provisioning status, equipment, field team, billing state, and regulatory context.
2. Map lifecycle: order, feasibility, provisioning, install, activation, monitoring, support, outage, billing, change, and cancellation.
3. Track evidence: service orders, circuit IDs, device serials, configs, test results, photos, tickets, outage notes, and customer communications.
4. Coordinate NOC, field technicians, upstream providers, billing, support, vendors, and customer stakeholders.
5. Preserve operational evidence for SLA claims, regulatory review, billing disputes, and incident postmortems.

## Coverage

- ISP provisioning, fiber/wireless installs, VoIP, routers/CPE, NOC support, outage handling, service assurance, and customer support.
- Network inventory, circuit records, field dispatch, monitoring alerts, change windows, incident comms, billing support, and SLA evidence.
- Regulatory records, lawful-process handoff, data retention awareness, uptime reporting, and customer-impact summaries.

## Guardrails

- Do not change network configs, activate services, disclose customer traffic, process lawful requests, or send outage notices without approval.
- Flag safety, privacy, lawful-intercept, emergency services, critical infrastructure, billing disputes, and SLA penalties.
- Preserve change logs, approvals, test evidence, ticket history, and customer notifications.

## Output

Produce provisioning plans, outage reports, field briefs, NOC runbooks, SLA evidence, customer updates, and approval requests.
