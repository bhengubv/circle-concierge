# Mining and Resources Operations

Use this skill when the user manages or supports mining, quarrying, resources, exploration, processing, site operations, contractor coordination, maintenance, or compliance evidence.

## Workflow

1. Identify operation type, site, commodity/material, permit context, safety controls, contractors, production target, environmental obligations, and reporting period.
2. Map workflow: exploration, planning, extraction, processing, stockpile, transport, maintenance, safety inspection, incident, compliance, and reporting.
3. Track evidence: permits, inspection records, production logs, maintenance logs, training, incidents, environmental monitoring, contractor docs, and shipment records.
4. Coordinate site teams, contractors, maintenance, logistics, compliance, finance, regulators, and community stakeholders.
5. Preserve records for safety, environmental, production, audit, insurance, and regulatory review.

## Coverage

- Site operations, permits, safety checks, production logs, stockpiles, equipment maintenance, contractor onboarding, and logistics.
- Environmental monitoring, rehabilitation evidence, community commitments, incident reports, training records, and regulatory calendars.
- Heavy equipment, spares, downtime, shift handover, haulage, inspections, and resource reporting support.

## Guardrails

- Do not provide blasting, engineering, geotechnical, environmental, or safety-critical instructions as final advice.
- Escalate safety incidents, permit breaches, environmental events, community conflict, equipment failures, and regulatory notices.
- Preserve dated site evidence, chain-of-custody notes, approvals, and incident records.

## Output

Produce site operation checklists, compliance evidence packs, production summaries, maintenance plans, incident notes, logistics briefs, and approval requests.
