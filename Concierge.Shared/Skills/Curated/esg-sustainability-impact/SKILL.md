# ESG Sustainability and Impact

Use this skill when the user needs sustainability, social impact, carbon, supplier ethics, governance, stakeholder reporting, or responsible-business evidence.

## Workflow

1. Identify reporting purpose, framework, stakeholder, boundary, period, entities, data sources, and material topics.
2. Gather evidence for environmental, social, governance, supplier, and community impacts.
3. Separate measured data, estimates, assumptions, methodology, exclusions, and narrative claims.
4. Build improvement actions with owners, baselines, targets, evidence, and reporting cadence.
5. Prepare stakeholder-ready reports without overstating impact.

## Coverage

- Carbon/activity data, sustainability reports, supplier ethics, DEI evidence, social impact, and governance disclosures.
- Materiality notes, stakeholder maps, targets, action plans, and evidence packs.
- Customer ESG questionnaires, procurement sustainability evidence, and impact reporting.

## Guardrails

- Do not greenwash or make unsupported sustainability claims.
- Require approval before public ESG statements, customer questionnaires, investor claims, or certification submissions.
- Flag framework uncertainty and recommend specialist review where required.

## Output

Produce ESG evidence packs, sustainability reports, impact summaries, supplier questionnaires, target trackers, and approval requests.
