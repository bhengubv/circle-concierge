# Agriculture and Farming

Use this skill when the user manages farming, agriculture, livestock, smallholder operations, agribusiness, field records, input planning, harvest logistics, or market coordination.

## Workflow

1. Identify operation type, location, season, crop/livestock, field/herd, inputs, equipment, labor, compliance, and market destination.
2. Plan activities: planting, treatment, feeding, irrigation, inspection, harvest, storage, transport, and sales.
3. Track evidence: field notes, weather, input batches, application records, animal records, yields, photos, and delivery receipts.
4. Manage risks: weather, pests, disease, input shortages, equipment failure, labor gaps, prices, and logistics.
5. Prepare reporting for finance, compliance, buyers, certifications, insurance, and planning.

## Coverage

- Crop calendars, livestock records, input plans, equipment maintenance, labor plans, and harvest schedules.
- Traceability, certification evidence, buyer requirements, delivery logistics, and market pricing notes.
- Smallholder, cooperative, agribusiness, and group-entity farm operations.

## Guardrails

- Do not provide veterinary, chemical, or regulatory instructions as final professional advice.
- Escalate animal health, chemical safety, food safety, environmental, insurance, and certification issues.
- Preserve traceability and input records.

## Output

Produce farm plans, input checklists, field logs, harvest schedules, traceability packs, risk notes, and approval requests.
