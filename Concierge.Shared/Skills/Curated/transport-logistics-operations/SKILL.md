# Transport and Logistics Operations

Use this skill when the user manages freight, fleet, warehousing, dispatch, courier/last-mile delivery, shipping, customs, carrier relationships, or logistics evidence.

## Workflow

1. Identify shipment/job type, origin, destination, carrier, vehicle, warehouse, service level, customs needs, customer promise, and risk.
2. Map lifecycle: quote, booking, pickup, warehouse handling, linehaul, customs, delivery, proof of delivery, exception, billing, and claim.
3. Track operational evidence: labels, manifests, tracking, PODs, photos, weights, dimensions, customs docs, invoices, and exception notes.
4. Monitor performance: OTIF, delivery attempts, delays, damages, costs, capacity, route efficiency, and carrier scorecards.
5. Gate high-risk actions: customer promises, customs declarations, claims, reroutes, refunds, and carrier changes.

## Coverage

- Fleet, freight, warehouse, dispatch, route planning, last-mile delivery, cross-border shipping, customs, returns, and carrier management.
- Parcel Ninja-style fulfillment, courier integrations, tracking events, delivery exceptions, inventory handoff, and proof-of-delivery workflows.
- Logistics cost analysis, service levels, claims, insurance evidence, driver/vehicle records, and customer notifications.

## Guardrails

- Do not submit customs declarations, reroute shipments, issue refunds, approve claims, or promise delivery dates without approval.
- Flag dangerous goods, customs risk, theft, damage, insurance, customer-impacting delays, and regulatory transport issues.
- Preserve shipment evidence, tracking events, communications, and exception approvals.

## Output

Produce logistics plans, dispatch briefs, tracking summaries, exception reports, carrier scorecards, claim packs, and approval requests.
