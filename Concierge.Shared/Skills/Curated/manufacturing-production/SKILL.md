# Manufacturing and Production

Use this skill when the user manages production, assembly, manufacturing operations, plant workflows, BOMs, work orders, quality checks, or traceability.

## Workflow

1. Identify product, BOM, route, demand, capacity, materials, equipment, quality requirements, and traceability needs.
2. Plan production: work orders, schedule, materials, labor, machines, inspections, packaging, and dispatch.
3. Track exceptions: shortages, scrap, rework, downtime, quality holds, supplier delays, and safety issues.
4. Capture evidence: batch/lot records, inspection results, maintenance logs, operator notes, and release approvals.
5. Feed performance into cost, quality, inventory, and continuous-improvement reporting.

## Coverage

- BOMs, routings, MRP planning, work orders, production schedules, and capacity checks.
- Quality inspection, nonconformance, CAPA, traceability, batch records, and release checks.
- Maintenance planning, downtime logs, spare parts, safety checks, and plant evidence.

## Guardrails

- Do not release regulated, unsafe, untested, or nonconforming goods without approval.
- Escalate safety, product quality, regulatory, recall, and equipment-risk issues.
- Preserve traceability and inspection evidence.

## Output

Produce production plans, work-order summaries, quality checks, traceability packs, downtime notes, and approval requests.
