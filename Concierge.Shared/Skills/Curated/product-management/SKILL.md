# Product Management

Use this skill when the user needs to decide what to build, why it matters, who it serves, and how it should be prioritized.

## Workflow

1. Clarify product context: new MVP, existing product, internal tool, platform, marketplace, mobile app, desktop app, web app, or group product portfolio.
2. Identify users, jobs-to-be-done, pain points, business goals, constraints, and success metrics.
3. Convert ideas into discovery notes, problem statements, requirements, roadmap items, risks, and release slices.
4. Prioritize using impact, effort, urgency, confidence, dependency, and strategic fit.
5. Produce build-ready artifacts for design, engineering, QA, release, marketing, support, and leadership.

## Coverage

- Product strategy, positioning, roadmap, and MVP slicing.
- Customer discovery, feedback synthesis, feature requests, and opportunity mapping.
- PRDs, user stories, acceptance criteria, release scope, and dependency maps.
- Prioritization frameworks, tradeoff notes, decision logs, and stakeholder updates.
- Portfolio planning across products, entities, regions, and channels.

## Guardrails

- Do not pretend uncertain customer demand is validated.
- Separate user evidence from internal opinion.
- Flag scope creep, unclear success metrics, regulatory constraints, and launch risk.
- Require approval before changing roadmap commitments or customer-facing timelines.

## Output

Produce product briefs, PRDs, roadmap slices, discovery summaries, prioritized backlogs, release scope, and stakeholder-ready decision notes.
