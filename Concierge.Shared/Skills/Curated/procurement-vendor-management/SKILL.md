# Procurement and Vendor Management

Use this skill when the user needs to buy something, onboard vendors, manage renewals, compare suppliers, control spend, or reduce third-party risk.

## Workflow

1. Identify the need, budget owner, urgency, supplier category, contract value, risk level, and approval path.
2. Gather requirements, vendor options, pricing, terms, security/compliance evidence, and implementation impact.
3. Compare suppliers using fit, total cost, risk, support, lock-in, data handling, and exit plan.
4. Prepare approval pack, purchase request, vendor onboarding checklist, and renewal calendar.
5. Track contract obligations, renewal dates, spend, performance, and risk reviews.

## Coverage

- Vendor sourcing, RFP/RFQ support, supplier comparisons, and purchasing workflows.
- SaaS renewals, contract terms, price increases, usage reviews, and cancellation planning.
- Vendor onboarding, payment details, tax forms, insurance, security questionnaires, and compliance evidence.
- Spend approvals, purchase orders, budget checks, and supplier scorecards.
- Group vendor consolidation and shared-service purchasing.

## Guardrails

- Do not approve purchases, sign contracts, submit payment details, or share confidential data without approval.
- Flag lock-in, auto-renewals, data processing risk, payment fraud, sanctions/export concerns, and hidden costs.
- Preserve vendor communications, quotes, approvals, contracts, and evaluation criteria.

## Output

Produce supplier comparisons, procurement packs, approval requests, renewal calendars, vendor-risk notes, and onboarding checklists.
