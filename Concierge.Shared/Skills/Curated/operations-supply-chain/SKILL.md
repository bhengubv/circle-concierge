# Operations and Supply Chain

Use this skill when the user is managing operational execution: procurement, vendors, inventory, logistics, fulfillment, service delivery, facilities, quality controls, or process improvement.

## Workflow

1. Identify the operating model: digital service, ecommerce, physical goods, marketplace, agency, group service center, or hybrid.
2. Map the workflow from demand to delivery: request, approval, procurement, production, fulfillment, delivery, support, and reconciliation.
3. Capture constraints: supplier lead times, inventory, capacity, budget, location, compliance, customer promises, and service levels.
4. Define controls: approvals, handoffs, evidence, exception handling, stock checks, quality gates, and cleanup.
5. Produce an executable operating plan with owners, dates, dependencies, and risks.

## Coverage

- Vendor onboarding, procurement requests, purchase orders, and supplier scorecards.
- Inventory, fulfillment, returns, logistics, delivery status, and warehouse/service workflows.
- Ecommerce and marketplace operations for Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, AliExpress, Taobao, Takealot, logistics partners, and Parcel Ninja-style fulfillment.
- Internal operating cadence, SOPs, quality checks, incident response, and continuous improvement.
- Group operations across departments, entities, regions, and shared services.

## Guardrails

- Do not place orders, change delivery promises, approve vendors, or trigger fulfillment without approval.
- Highlight operational risk around stockouts, delays, compliance, customs, fragile goods, and customer commitments.
- Preserve vendor communications, purchase evidence, tracking numbers, and exception decisions.

## Output

Produce SOPs, procurement packs, vendor comparisons, fulfillment plans, exception logs, operating dashboards, and approval requests.
