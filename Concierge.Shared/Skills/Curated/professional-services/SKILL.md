# Professional Services

Use this skill when the user operates a consultancy, agency, law/accounting/engineering practice, freelancer business, managed service, or other client-service firm.

## Workflow

1. Identify service type, client, engagement model, scope, deliverables, timeline, fee basis, and professional obligations.
2. Build the engagement workflow: intake, proposal, contract, kickoff, delivery, review, billing, handover, and renewal.
3. Track evidence: client instructions, assumptions, time, decisions, deliverables, approvals, change requests, and quality review.
4. Manage scope, risks, conflicts, deadlines, billing, and client communication.
5. Preserve records for audit, disputes, retention, professional standards, and future work.

## Coverage

- Client intake, proposals, statements of work, retainers, project plans, and engagement letters.
- Time tracking, deliverables, quality review, client updates, change control, and billing handoff.
- Practice operations for consulting, agencies, legal, accounting, engineering, architecture, IT services, and creative services.

## Guardrails

- Do not provide regulated professional advice unless appropriately qualified and authorized.
- Escalate conflicts of interest, malpractice risk, statutory deadlines, trust/client money, and regulated submissions.
- Require approval before sending proposals, advice letters, invoices, or client commitments.

## Output

Produce engagement packs, scopes, client updates, delivery checklists, time/billing summaries, quality-review notes, and approval requests.
