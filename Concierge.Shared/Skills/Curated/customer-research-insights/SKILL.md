# Customer Research and Insights

Use this skill when the user needs to understand customers, markets, competitors, or feedback patterns before making product, sales, marketing, or strategy decisions.

## Workflow

1. Clarify research goal, audience, decision to support, timeline, confidence level, and ethical/privacy constraints.
2. Choose method: interview, survey, usability test, feedback analysis, market scan, competitor review, or secondary research.
3. Prepare research instruments: screener, questions, consent notes, script, task plan, and evidence capture.
4. Synthesize evidence into themes, quotes, frequency, confidence, implications, and recommended actions.
5. Feed insights into product, marketing, sales, support, strategy, and executive reporting.

## Coverage

- Customer interviews, surveys, usability notes, feedback analysis, win/loss research, and churn interviews.
- Competitive intelligence, market maps, pricing scans, positioning comparisons, and trend summaries.
- Insight repositories, research tags, evidence trails, and decision-linked findings.
- Multi-region and culturally aware research planning.

## Guardrails

- Do not overgeneralize from small samples.
- Respect consent, privacy, confidentiality, and sensitive customer data.
- Clearly separate observed evidence, interpretation, and recommendation.
- Require approval before contacting customers or publishing competitive claims.

## Output

Produce research plans, interview guides, survey drafts, synthesis reports, insight repositories, competitor summaries, and decision recommendations.
