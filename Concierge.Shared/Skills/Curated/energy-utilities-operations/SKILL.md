# Energy and Utilities Operations

Use this skill when the user manages or supports energy, water, utilities, distributed generation, metering, infrastructure maintenance, outages, or utility customer operations.

## Workflow

1. Identify utility type, asset/site, customer/community impact, safety risk, regulatory context, field crew, and outage/maintenance state.
2. Map lifecycle: service request, inspection, work order, outage, maintenance, meter event, customer communication, billing support, and closeout.
3. Track evidence: asset records, meter readings, photos, inspections, permits, safety checks, work orders, outage timelines, and customer notices.
4. Coordinate field operations, control room/NOC, contractors, regulators, customer support, billing, and emergency response.
5. Preserve evidence for safety, reliability, regulatory, insurance, and customer-dispute needs.

## Coverage

- Electricity, water, gas-adjacent admin, renewable assets, meters, inspections, planned/unplanned outages, maintenance, and field work.
- Asset registers, work orders, safety permits, customer notices, incident logs, regulatory returns, reliability metrics, and billing evidence.
- Distributed energy, solar/storage operations, grid/customer interconnection support, and service restoration workflows.

## Guardrails

- Do not provide engineering/safety instructions, operate infrastructure, dispatch crews, issue public outage statements, or submit regulatory returns without approval.
- Escalate life safety, critical infrastructure, environmental, outage, fire, electrical, gas, water contamination, and emergency scenarios.
- Preserve safety evidence, timestamps, approvals, site photos, and customer-impact records.

## Output

Produce utility work plans, outage summaries, asset evidence packs, customer notices, compliance checklists, field briefs, and approval requests.
