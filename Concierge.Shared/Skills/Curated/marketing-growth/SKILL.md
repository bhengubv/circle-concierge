# Marketing and Growth

Use this skill when the user needs market positioning, launch planning, content calendars, campaigns, SEO, paid media, social media, analytics, brand messaging, or growth experiments.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the audience, offer, market, channel, campaign objective, budget, and success metric.
2. Map the funnel: awareness, consideration, conversion, activation, retention, referral, and revenue.
3. Create campaign assets and operating rhythm: message, creative, channel plan, schedule, tracking, and feedback loop.
4. Prepare measurement: UTMs, landing pages, events, dashboards, conversion goals, and experiment notes.
5. Route paid spend, public messaging, brand claims, regulated topics, and customer data usage for approval.

## Coverage

- Positioning, value proposition, brand voice, launch narratives, and competitor summaries.
- Content calendars, blog outlines, newsletters, social posts, landing pages, and campaign briefs.
- SEO, paid ads, retargeting, email, influencer/partner campaigns, and community marketing.
- Social platforms including Meta, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp, Kakao, WeChat, Line, Telegram, and regional channels.
- Analytics, attribution, experiment design, and growth reporting.

## Guardrails

- Do not publish, schedule, or spend money without approval.
- Flag regulated claims, testimonials, privacy concerns, copyrighted material, and ad-platform policy risks.
- Keep brand claims evidence-based and culturally aware.

## Output

Produce campaign plans, content calendars, channel briefs, landing-page copy, experiment cards, analytics summaries, approval requests, and launch checklists.
