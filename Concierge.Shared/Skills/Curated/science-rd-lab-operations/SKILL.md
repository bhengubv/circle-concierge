# Science R&D and Lab Operations

Use this skill when the user manages research, lab operations, experiments, prototypes, samples, protocols, equipment, scientific evidence, or R&D programs.

## Workflow

1. Identify research goal, lab context, sample/material type, protocol status, safety requirements, data requirements, and review authority.
2. Map workflow: hypothesis, protocol, materials, equipment, run plan, observations, data capture, analysis, review, storage, and reporting.
3. Track evidence: lab notebook entries, sample IDs, chain of custody, instrument logs, calibration, deviations, photos, datasets, and approvals.
4. Coordinate researchers, lab managers, safety officers, QA/compliance, vendors, sponsors, and collaborators.
5. Preserve data integrity, reproducibility, safety evidence, and IP/confidentiality records.

## Coverage

- Experiments, protocols, lab notebooks, sample tracking, instrument/equipment records, calibration, safety checks, and research data.
- R&D portfolios, prototype evidence, literature/research synthesis, grants, collaboration notes, IP handoff, and reporting.
- GLP/GxP-adjacent evidence awareness, data integrity, audit trails, controlled documents, and deviation/CAPA handoff.

## Guardrails

- Do not provide hazardous, medical, biosecurity, chemical, or regulated experimental instructions as final expert advice.
- Escalate safety, ethics, animal/human subjects, biohazard, chemical, radiation, clinical, IP, and regulatory matters.
- Preserve protocol versions, raw data, approvals, sample records, deviations, and audit trails.

## Output

Produce experiment plans, protocol checklists, lab evidence packs, sample trackers, research summaries, deviation notes, and approval requests.
