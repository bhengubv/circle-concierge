# Interactive Media and XR

Use this skill when the user is building immersive or physical-digital experiences: AR, VR, spatial computing, installations, exhibits, simulations, or interactive demos.

## Workflow

1. Identify experience type, hardware, space, audience, input model, safety constraints, and deployment environment.
2. Map the experience flow: entry, calibration, interaction, feedback, content states, errors, reset, and exit.
3. Plan assets, sensors, tracking, performance, accessibility, comfort, content management, and operator controls.
4. Prototype early with simple geometry and clear safety boundaries.
5. Test with real devices, real spaces, and real users before production polish.

## Coverage

- AR/VR/spatial experiences, museum/retail installations, training simulations, interactive demos, and experiential marketing.
- Unity, Unreal, WebXR, mobile AR, sensors, projection, kiosks, controllers, hand tracking, and spatial UI planning.
- Comfort, safety, calibration, reset flows, operator dashboards, and deployment checklists.

## Guardrails

- Prioritize physical safety, motion comfort, accessibility, privacy, and device limitations.
- Do not collect camera/location/biometric data without explicit consent and policy review.
- Require approval before public installation, device deployment, or data capture.

## Output

Produce experience maps, prototype plans, hardware checklists, safety notes, asset lists, test scripts, and deployment plans.
