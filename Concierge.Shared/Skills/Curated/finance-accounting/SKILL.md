# Finance and Accounting

Use this skill when the user is dealing with money, invoices, bookkeeping, cash flow, budgets, tax readiness, accounting systems, payment evidence, or financial operating controls.

## Workflow

1. Identify the financial context: startup, existing entity, group entity, sole proprietor, nonprofit, or project cost center.
2. Separate operational finance from regulated advice: bookkeeping, reporting, and evidence can be assisted; tax/legal/accounting decisions should be approval-gated and professional-review friendly.
3. Gather required records: invoices, receipts, bank feeds, payment confirmations, contracts, payroll records, tax numbers, and accounting exports.
4. Reconcile the objective: cash position, runway, AR/AP, VAT/GST/sales-tax readiness, management accounts, budget variance, or audit pack.
5. Produce a decision-ready output with assumptions, missing evidence, risks, and next actions.

## Coverage

- Chart of accounts planning and cleanup.
- Invoice, quote, purchase order, receipt, and payment tracking.
- Accounts receivable and accounts payable workflows.
- Cash flow, runway, burn, budget, and variance reporting.
- Month-end close, reconciliations, and evidence packs.
- Tax/VAT/GST readiness checklists without pretending to be a tax practitioner.
- Xero, QuickBooks, Sage, Zoho Books, Stripe, PayPal, bank-feed, and payment-provider integration planning.
- Group entity consolidation, intercompany notes, and entity-level reporting boundaries.

## Guardrails

- Do not submit tax filings, make payments, move money, or change accounting records without explicit approval.
- Surface jurisdiction, currency, entity, and period assumptions.
- Keep an audit trail for calculations, source documents, and external-system changes.
- Recommend professional review for tax positions, statutory accounts, payroll compliance, and regulated financial advice.

## Output

Produce finance-ready schedules, checklists, journal/evidence suggestions, variance summaries, reconciliations, approval requests, and integration action plans.
