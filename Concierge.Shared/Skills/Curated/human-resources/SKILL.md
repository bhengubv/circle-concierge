# Human Resources

Use this skill when the user needs help with people operations: hiring, onboarding, employee records, contractor setup, leave, performance, policies, training, payroll coordination, or offboarding.

## Workflow

1. Identify worker type: founder, employee, contractor, advisor, intern, vendor, or group-company shared resource.
2. Clarify jurisdiction, entity, role, start date, employment basis, compensation model, and required approvals.
3. Build the people workflow: requisition, offer, contract pack, onboarding, equipment, access, policy acknowledgement, payroll handoff, and review cadence.
4. Track evidence: signed agreements, identity/right-to-work checks where applicable, emergency contacts, role descriptions, training logs, leave records, and policy acknowledgements.
5. Separate administrative drafting from legal/employment advice and route high-risk actions for review.

## Coverage

- Hiring plans, job descriptions, scorecards, interview kits, and offer checklists.
- Employee and contractor onboarding.
- HR policy drafts for leave, remote work, conduct, data handling, expenses, and equipment.
- Performance goals, one-on-one templates, review packs, and improvement plans.
- Leave, attendance, payroll handoff, and benefits coordination.
- Offboarding, access removal, equipment return, and exit evidence.
- Multi-entity workforce routing for group structures.

## Guardrails

- Do not make employment-law claims as final advice.
- Escalate terminations, disciplinary action, discrimination, harassment, immigration, payroll tax, and benefits compliance for professional review.
- Require approval before sending offers, contracts, policy notices, payroll changes, or termination communications.
- Protect sensitive personal data and apply least-privilege access.

## Output

Produce HR-ready workflows, templates, checklists, role packs, onboarding plans, people-risk summaries, and approval-gated communication drafts.
