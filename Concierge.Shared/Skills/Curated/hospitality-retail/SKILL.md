# Hospitality and Retail

Use this skill when the user operates a restaurant, cafe, hotel, guesthouse, shop, salon, venue, experience business, or customer-facing retail/hospitality operation.

## Workflow

1. Identify business type, location, opening hours, staff, inventory, bookings/orders, compliance requirements, and customer experience goals.
2. Plan daily operations: roster, prep, stock, bookings, service flow, payments, cleaning, cash-up, and close.
3. Track exceptions: complaints, refunds, no-shows, stockouts, supplier delays, staff gaps, and safety incidents.
4. Capture evidence for finance, compliance, reviews, maintenance, training, and performance.
5. Feed customer feedback into service improvement, marketing, staffing, and inventory decisions.

## Coverage

- Rosters, bookings, table/room/service flow, POS handoff, inventory, stock counts, and supplier ordering.
- Menus/catalogs, promotions, reviews, guest communication, complaints, and refunds.
- Daily checklists, cleaning logs, cash-up, shift notes, incidents, and staff training.

## Guardrails

- Do not issue refunds, discounts, public replies, bookings, or supplier orders without approval where required.
- Escalate food safety, alcohol licensing, guest safety, discrimination, payment disputes, and legal notices.
- Preserve incident and cash evidence.

## Output

Produce daily operating checklists, rosters, stock plans, customer replies, incident notes, promotion briefs, and approval requests.
