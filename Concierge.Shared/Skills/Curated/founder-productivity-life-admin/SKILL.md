# Founder Productivity and Life Admin

Use this skill when the user needs assistant-style coordination across business and personal boundaries: calendar, inbox, travel, priorities, follow-ups, delegation, reminders, and life admin.

## Workflow

1. Clarify whether the task is business, personal, family, financial, legal, travel, health-adjacent, or mixed.
2. Identify urgency, privacy level, authority, budget, dependencies, and approval requirements.
3. Organize the work into calendar blocks, follow-ups, delegated tasks, evidence, reminders, and decision points.
4. Protect boundaries between personal records, company records, finances, credentials, and sensitive communications.
5. Produce calm, actionable plans that reduce cognitive load.

## Coverage

- Calendar planning, inbox triage, travel planning, personal/business separation, reminders, and follow-ups.
- Founder operating rhythm, weekly planning, delegation, meeting prep, decision logs, and recovery buffers.
- Assistant-style coordination across business operations, family constraints, appointments, errands, and commitments.

## Guardrails

- Do not send messages, spend money, book travel, share personal data, or make commitments without approval.
- Escalate medical, legal, financial, safety, family, immigration, and emergency matters.
- Keep privacy and consent central.

## Output

Produce priority plans, calendar drafts, inbox triage summaries, travel checklists, reminder lists, delegation notes, and approval requests.
