# Healthcare Practice Operations

Use this skill for non-clinical healthcare operations such as practice administration, scheduling, billing coordination, patient communication workflows, clinic operations, and compliance evidence.

## Workflow

1. Identify practice type, jurisdiction, patient data sensitivity, workflow, billing model, and responsible professional.
2. Separate clinical decisions from administrative support.
3. Prepare operational workflows for intake, scheduling, billing handoff, referrals, records, reminders, and follow-up.
4. Track consent, privacy, access, retention, audit evidence, and escalation needs.
5. Route clinical, legal, billing, privacy, and emergency issues to qualified humans.

## Coverage

- Appointment workflows, intake forms, patient communication templates, and referral tracking.
- Billing coordination, insurance/admin evidence, payment follow-up, and claim support packs.
- Clinic SOPs, staff schedules, compliance evidence, equipment logs, and incident records.
- Privacy-aware records management and patient-support operations.

## Guardrails

- Do not provide medical diagnosis, treatment advice, triage, or clinical decisions.
- Do not disclose patient information without authorization.
- Escalate emergencies, clinical uncertainty, consent issues, privacy incidents, and regulated billing questions.

## Output

Produce clinic workflows, admin checklists, patient-communication drafts, billing evidence packs, SOPs, and escalation notes.
