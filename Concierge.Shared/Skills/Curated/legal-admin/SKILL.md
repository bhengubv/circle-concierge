# Legal and Administration

Use this skill when the user is handling company administration, entity setup, contracts, governance, government filings, licenses, regulatory evidence, or legal operations.

## Workflow

1. Identify the legal context: new company, existing entity, group entity, branch, nonprofit, trading name, partnership, or sole proprietor.
2. Determine jurisdiction, entity numbers, responsible officers, statutory deadlines, and required supporting documents.
3. Classify the action: draft, review checklist, filing prep, submission, renewal, contract workflow, board approval, or compliance evidence.
4. Prepare an approval-gated package with assumptions, documents, risks, signatures, fees, and submission route.
5. Keep durable records for audit, governance, and future due diligence.

## Coverage

- Business registration readiness and government/API submission planning.
- Company secretarial records, director/member/shareholder registers, resolutions, minutes, and annual returns.
- Contract intake, clause checklists, obligation tracking, renewal reminders, and signature packs.
- Licenses, permits, vendor onboarding, insurance evidence, and compliance calendars.
- Data processing agreements, NDAs, service agreements, terms, privacy policy, and acceptable-use workflow drafts.
- Group entity maps, intercompany agreements, authority matrices, and record boundaries.

## Guardrails

- Do not provide final legal advice or submit legal filings without explicit user approval.
- Highlight jurisdiction-specific uncertainty and recommend professional review for high-risk or regulated matters.
- Require approval before contract dispatch, signature requests, government submissions, payments, or authority changes.
- Preserve source documents, timestamps, submitter identity, and evidence bundles.

## Output

Produce legal/admin checklists, filing packs, contract review summaries, governance records, evidence bundles, approval requests, and professional-review briefs.
