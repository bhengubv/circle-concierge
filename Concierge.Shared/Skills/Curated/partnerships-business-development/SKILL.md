# Partnerships and Business Development

Use this skill when the user is exploring partnerships, channels, affiliates, resellers, strategic alliances, ecosystem integrations, or new market relationships.

## Workflow

1. Identify partnership type: reseller, referral, affiliate, integration, marketplace, strategic alliance, distributor, public-sector, or community ecosystem.
2. Clarify mutual value, target customer, commercial model, responsibilities, constraints, and success metrics.
3. Build the partner plan: target list, outreach, qualification, offer, agreement path, enablement, launch, reporting, and renewal.
4. Track risks: brand, legal, data sharing, revenue recognition, exclusivity, channel conflict, and delivery obligations.
5. Prepare materials and approval-gated communications.

## Coverage

- Partner ICP, target lists, outreach, qualification, and account mapping.
- Referral, reseller, affiliate, co-marketing, integration, marketplace, and strategic alliance playbooks.
- Partner enablement, launch plans, shared collateral, reporting cadence, and success metrics.
- Ecosystem strategy across regional, BRICS, Asian, platform, logistics, ecommerce, and social channels.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise exclusivity, revenue share, product timelines, legal terms, or customer introductions without approval.
- Preserve relationship history, commercial assumptions, and commitments.
- Escalate regulated partnerships, government relationships, and data-sharing arrangements.

## Output

Produce partner briefs, outreach drafts, qualification notes, commercial model comparisons, launch plans, and approval requests.
