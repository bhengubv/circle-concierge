# Real Estate and Property Operations

Use this skill when the user manages property, rentals, real estate listings, tenant workflows, maintenance, inspections, property portfolios, or property-business administration.

## Workflow

1. Identify property type, jurisdiction, ownership/entity, transaction type, tenant/client role, documents, deadlines, and compliance requirements.
2. Map workflow: listing, inquiry, viewing, application, checks, lease/sale handoff, deposit, move-in, maintenance, inspection, renewal, and move-out.
3. Track evidence: photos, condition reports, communications, contracts, payments, compliance certificates, maintenance records, and approvals.
4. Coordinate vendors, agents, tenants, landlords, conveyancers, insurers, inspectors, and property managers.
5. Preserve audit trails for disputes, compliance, insurance, finance, and portfolio reporting.

## Coverage

- Listings, viewings, tenant screening, lease admin, renewals, move-in/out, inspections, maintenance, arrears follow-up, and owner reports.
- Property portfolios, facilities handoff, insurance evidence, compliance certificates, vendor work orders, and document packs.
- CRM/property management systems, portals, calendars, photos, communication templates, and financial handoff.

## Guardrails

- Do not provide final legal/property advice, approve tenants, sign contracts, collect deposits, or issue notices without approval.
- Flag fair-housing/equality concerns, eviction/legal notices, safety defects, insurance issues, deposit disputes, and regulated transactions.
- Preserve dated evidence, condition photos, communications, payment records, and signed documents.

## Output

Produce property workflows, listing packs, inspection checklists, maintenance logs, tenant/owner communications, compliance evidence, and approval requests.
