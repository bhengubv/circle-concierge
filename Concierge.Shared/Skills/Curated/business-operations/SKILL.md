# Business Operations

Use this skill when the user wants to coordinate business functions, operating rhythms, startup setup, group entities, compliance, or cross-functional work.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the entity context: new venture, existing company, group structure, or trading name.
2. Identify the business function: product, sales, marketing, support, finance, legal/admin, operations, or engineering.
3. Convert goals into workstreams, evidence, decisions, and next actions.
4. Surface required approvals before external submissions, payments, filings, or customer communication.
5. Track evidence needed for audits, compliance, finance, and release readiness.

## Operating Principles

- Concierge should help a one-person startup operate like a small team.
- Existing entities should be able to import evidence and hit the ground running.
- Government/API workflows should be guided and approval-gated.
- Financial, legal, HR, medical, and regulated actions require extra care and explicit confirmation.

## Output

Produce concrete operating plans, checklists, evidence requirements, and responsible next actions.
