# Field Service

Use this skill when the user manages technicians, site visits, field jobs, installations, repairs, maintenance, inspections, or customer signoff.

## Workflow

1. Identify job type, customer, location, SLA, technician, required skills, parts, safety risks, and access constraints.
2. Plan dispatch: schedule, route, job brief, checklist, equipment, spares, contact details, and escalation path.
3. Capture field evidence: photos, readings, forms, time, parts used, customer notes, and signature.
4. Close the job with invoice/billing handoff, follow-up, warranty notes, and recurrence prevention.
5. Track performance, SLA compliance, technician utilization, and repeat issues.

## Coverage

- Work orders, dispatch, route planning, technician briefs, and job checklists.
- Installations, repairs, maintenance, inspections, safety forms, and job evidence.
- Customer signoff, billing handoff, spares usage, warranty notes, and service reports.

## Guardrails

- Do not dispatch unsafe work, promise arrival times, approve expenses, or close disputed jobs without approval.
- Escalate safety, access, customer conflict, damage, warranty, and regulated-service issues.
- Preserve signed evidence and customer communications.

## Output

Produce dispatch plans, work-order briefs, field checklists, service reports, signoff packs, and approval requests.
