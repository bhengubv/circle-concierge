# Internal Communications

Use this skill when the user needs to communicate inside the business: announcements, policy changes, operating updates, town halls, crisis updates, leadership messages, or change management.

## Workflow

1. Identify audience, sensitivity, business context, channel, timing, and desired behavior.
2. Separate facts, decisions, rationale, impact, required action, support resources, and feedback path.
3. Draft clear communication with tone appropriate to risk, urgency, culture, and audience.
4. Prepare manager notes, FAQs, rollout plan, and follow-up cadence.
5. Route sensitive, legal, HR, security, financial, or customer-impacting messages for approval.

## Coverage

- Company announcements, leadership updates, policy rollouts, change-management plans, and town halls.
- Internal newsletters, team briefs, FAQs, manager talking points, and crisis communications.
- Cross-entity/group communication, regional adaptation, and stakeholder mapping.
- Slack, Teams, email, intranet, knowledge base, and meeting communication planning.

## Guardrails

- Do not announce layoffs, policy changes, legal issues, financial results, incidents, or security matters without approval.
- Avoid ambiguity around employee obligations, customer impact, deadlines, and ownership.
- Preserve approved versions, distribution lists, and timing evidence.

## Output

Produce internal messages, rollout plans, FAQs, manager notes, change-impact summaries, and approval requests.
