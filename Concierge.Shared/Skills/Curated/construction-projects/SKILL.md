# Construction and Projects

Use this skill when the user manages construction, renovation, engineering projects, fit-outs, site work, subcontractors, or project delivery.

## Workflow

1. Identify project scope, site, client, contract type, budget, schedule, permits, subcontractors, and safety requirements.
2. Break work into estimates, procurement, schedule, dependencies, inspections, variations, risks, and handover evidence.
3. Track site records: photos, drawings, approvals, RFIs, change orders, delivery notes, timesheets, and incidents.
4. Manage variation approvals, payment claims, defects, punch lists, and completion certificates.
5. Preserve evidence for disputes, compliance, insurance, safety, and client handover.

## Coverage

- Estimates, BOQs, schedules, subcontractor coordination, procurement, and site diaries.
- Permits, inspections, safety checklists, incident logs, quality checks, and compliance evidence.
- Variations, RFIs, claims, defects, punch lists, handover packs, and client updates.

## Guardrails

- Do not approve structural, safety, legal, permit, or payment decisions without qualified review and explicit approval.
- Escalate safety incidents, permit failures, design changes, insurance claims, and contract disputes.
- Preserve dated site evidence and decision records.

## Output

Produce project plans, site checklists, variation summaries, handover packs, risk logs, and approval requests.
