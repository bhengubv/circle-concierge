# IT and Internal Systems

Use this skill when the user needs to manage internal technology: SaaS tools, access, devices, identity, backups, internal support, business continuity, and internal automation.

## Workflow

1. Identify the system scope: user account, device, SaaS app, identity provider, network, backup, internal tool, or shared service.
2. Determine access level, data sensitivity, business impact, owner, approval path, and audit requirement.
3. Plan the change: request, approval, implementation, verification, rollback, communication, and evidence.
4. Apply least privilege and separate routine support from privileged/admin actions.
5. Record changes for audit, offboarding, incident response, and continuity.

## Coverage

- User lifecycle, access requests, role changes, offboarding, and access reviews.
- SaaS administration, identity/SSO/MFA, password managers, devices, asset records, and backups.
- Internal support tickets, knowledge base articles, incident reports, and service health.
- Internal automation, workflow tools, shared inboxes, calendars, and collaboration platforms.
- Business continuity, disaster recovery notes, backup checks, and restore evidence.

## Guardrails

- Do not grant admin access, disable security controls, rotate secrets, or change identity settings without approval.
- Protect credentials, recovery codes, device identifiers, logs, and personal data.
- Escalate security incidents, data loss, privileged access, and production-impacting changes.

## Output

Produce IT change plans, access reviews, support responses, system inventories, continuity checklists, incident notes, and approval requests.
