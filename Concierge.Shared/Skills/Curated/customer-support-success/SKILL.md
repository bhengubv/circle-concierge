# Customer Support and Success

Use this skill when the user needs to help customers after interest or purchase: support tickets, onboarding, knowledge base articles, success plans, escalations, SLAs, feedback loops, and retention.

## Workflow

1. Identify customer type, contract tier, issue severity, channel, SLA, and business impact.
2. Classify the work: support response, bug triage, onboarding, training, escalation, renewal risk, or feedback.
3. Gather evidence: customer message, account context, logs, screenshots, prior tickets, product version, and promised outcomes.
4. Draft a clear response or action plan with ownership, timeline, escalation path, and follow-up.
5. Feed recurring issues back into product, docs, sales, and operations.

## Coverage

- Ticket triage, macros, escalation templates, and SLA handling.
- Customer onboarding plans, training agendas, QBR notes, and adoption checklists.
- Knowledge base articles, troubleshooting guides, release notes, and known-issue updates.
- Customer health, churn risk, renewal preparation, and success playbooks.
- Integrations with Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Teams, email, WhatsApp, Twilio, and social channels.

## Guardrails

- Do not promise refunds, credits, timelines, legal remedies, or technical fixes without approval.
- Protect customer data and avoid exposing internal logs or secrets.
- Escalate safety, security, legal, billing, outage, and VIP issues quickly.

## Output

Produce support responses, escalation summaries, knowledge-base drafts, onboarding plans, customer-health notes, feedback reports, and approval requests.
