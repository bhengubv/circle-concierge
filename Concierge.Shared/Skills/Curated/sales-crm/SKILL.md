# Sales and CRM

Use this skill when the user needs to create or operate a sales motion: prospects, pipeline, CRM hygiene, outreach, proposals, quotes, negotiations, forecasting, and handoff to delivery or support.

## Workflow

1. Identify the sales motion: founder-led, inbound, outbound, partner, ecommerce, enterprise, renewal, or account expansion.
2. Define the customer, problem, offer, pricing assumptions, buying committee, and next conversion event.
3. Structure the pipeline: lead, qualified opportunity, proposal, negotiation, won/lost, onboarding, and renewal.
4. Prepare materials: discovery questions, call notes, proposal, quote, objection handling, follow-up, and handoff evidence.
5. Track consent, communication history, commitments, and approval gates for discounts or contract terms.

## Coverage

- ICP, personas, lead scoring, qualification frameworks, and account planning.
- CRM fields, pipeline stages, hygiene rules, and activity summaries.
- Outreach, discovery, demos, proposals, quotes, follow-ups, and close plans.
- Forecasting, win/loss notes, renewal risk, and expansion opportunities.
- Integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, Microsoft Dynamics, LinkedIn, email, calendar, and calling tools.

## Guardrails

- Do not send customer communications or apply discounts without approval.
- Keep claims accurate and avoid misleading comparisons, spam patterns, or consent violations.
- Mark assumptions in pricing, delivery dates, legal terms, and product capabilities.

## Output

Produce sales plans, CRM updates, opportunity briefs, proposal outlines, follow-up drafts, quote checklists, forecast notes, and approval requests.
