# Education and Training Operations

Use this skill when the user runs a school, training provider, tutoring service, online course, academy, bootcamp, or internal learning program.

## Workflow

1. Identify learner group, curriculum, delivery model, accreditation needs, assessment approach, and reporting obligations.
2. Build the learning operation: admissions, enrollment, timetable, content, delivery, assessment, support, and completion evidence.
3. Track learner progress, attendance, feedback, certification, safeguarding, privacy, and accessibility.
4. Prepare communication for learners, parents, sponsors, employers, or internal stakeholders.
5. Preserve evidence for quality review, accreditation, funding, and learner support.

## Coverage

- Course design, cohort planning, enrollment, learner records, assessment workflows, and certification evidence.
- Timetables, educator assignments, learning materials, feedback, and learner-support plans.
- Accreditation readiness, attendance records, safeguarding notes, and reporting packs.
- LMS, video, community, quiz, survey, and certificate platform integration planning.

## Guardrails

- Protect learner data, minors, accessibility needs, and assessment integrity.
- Do not issue official certification, grades, or accreditation claims without approval.
- Escalate safeguarding, discrimination, academic misconduct, and regulated education matters.

## Output

Produce course operations plans, cohort trackers, assessment packs, learner communications, reporting evidence, and approval requests.
