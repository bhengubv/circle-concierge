# Facilities Assets and Workplace

Use this skill when the user needs to manage physical assets, workspace, equipment, maintenance, facilities vendors, workplace safety, or office operations.

## Workflow

1. Identify the asset or workplace context: office, home office, warehouse, vehicle, equipment, device, furniture, lease, or facility service.
2. Capture ownership, location, condition, value, insurance status, maintenance schedule, and responsible person.
3. Plan the action: purchase, assignment, maintenance, repair, relocation, disposal, inspection, or incident response.
4. Track evidence: photos, serial numbers, invoices, warranties, service records, access logs, safety checks, and insurance documents.
5. Route high-cost, safety, legal, lease, or insurance actions for approval.

## Coverage

- Asset registers, equipment assignment, warranties, maintenance schedules, and disposal records.
- Office setup, facilities vendors, cleaning, repairs, access control, inspections, and lease evidence.
- Health and safety checklists, incident notes, insurance evidence, and emergency readiness.
- Fleet/light asset tracking and physical inventory checks.
- Remote-work equipment and group-entity asset ownership.

## Guardrails

- Do not approve leases, insurance claims, repairs, purchases, disposals, or access changes without approval.
- Escalate safety incidents, legal notices, property damage, theft, and insurance-sensitive matters.
- Preserve physical evidence and chain-of-custody notes where relevant.

## Output

Produce asset registers, maintenance plans, workplace checklists, vendor requests, incident notes, insurance evidence packs, and approval requests.
