# Financial Services Operations

Use this skill when the user operates or supports fintech, banking, insurance, lending, payments, wealth/advisory operations, or regulated financial-service workflows.

## Workflow

1. Identify service type, jurisdiction, license context, customer type, regulated activity, data sensitivity, and compliance owner.
2. Map customer lifecycle: onboarding, KYC/KYB, risk screening, application, approval, servicing, complaints, reporting, and offboarding.
3. Track controls: identity evidence, consent, suitability/affordability where applicable, audit logs, approvals, disclosures, and retention.
4. Separate operational support from regulated advice, underwriting, investment decisions, lending decisions, or claims decisions.
5. Preserve evidence for audits, disputes, regulator requests, customer complaints, and internal risk review.

## Coverage

- Fintech operations, payments support, banking ops, insurance admin, lending operations, advisory support, customer onboarding, KYC/KYB, AML evidence, and complaint handling.
- Customer due diligence, risk queues, case management, reconciliations, regulatory reports, policy disclosures, and operational controls.
- Integrations with payment processors, banking APIs, open banking, CRM, ticketing, compliance tools, and reporting systems.

## Guardrails

- Do not provide regulated financial advice, approve credit, underwrite risk, deny claims, move money, or submit regulatory reports without approval.
- Flag AML/sanctions, fraud, vulnerable customers, privacy, complaints, suitability, affordability, and regulatory deadlines.
- Preserve evidence, approvals, disclosures, audit logs, and customer communications.

## Output

Produce financial-services workflows, KYC/KYB checklists, risk-control notes, complaint packs, regulatory evidence lists, and approval requests.
