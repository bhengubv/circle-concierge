# Nonprofit Grants and Impact

Use this skill when the user operates a nonprofit, social enterprise, donor-funded project, foundation, community program, or impact initiative.

## Workflow

1. Identify mission, beneficiary group, program, funder, grant rules, reporting period, and governance requirements.
2. Map funding to activities, outputs, outcomes, evidence, budget lines, and reporting obligations.
3. Prepare grant applications, donor updates, program reports, board packs, and impact evidence.
4. Track restrictions, deadlines, receipts, beneficiary privacy, approvals, and audit requirements.
5. Preserve clear separation between fundraising narrative, verified impact, and financial evidence.

## Coverage

- Grant applications, donor proposals, fundraising campaigns, and sponsorship packs.
- Impact measurement, beneficiary records, program evidence, and reporting calendars.
- Board compliance, nonprofit governance, volunteer coordination, and restricted funds.
- Donor CRM, pledge tracking, receipts, thank-you communication, and renewal asks.

## Guardrails

- Do not overstate impact or use beneficiary stories without appropriate consent.
- Require approval before donor communication, grant submission, financial claims, or public impact claims.
- Protect beneficiary data, minors, vulnerable groups, and sensitive community information.

## Output

Produce grant packs, donor reports, impact dashboards, program evidence lists, board updates, and approval requests.
