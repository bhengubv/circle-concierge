# Media Publishing and Content Operations

Use this skill when the user manages content businesses, publishers, creators, agencies, editorial operations, newsletters, podcasts, video channels, courses, or media distribution.

## Workflow

1. Identify content type, audience, channel, editorial owner, rights status, monetization model, publishing cadence, and approval path.
2. Map production: idea, brief, research, draft, edit, legal/brand review, assets, scheduling, publishing, distribution, analytics, and archive.
3. Track rights and evidence: licenses, releases, sources, attribution, usage terms, claims, sponsor obligations, and version history.
4. Coordinate channels: website, newsletter, podcast, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Meta, streaming, LMS, syndication, and partners.
5. Measure performance and feed insights into editorial, product, marketing, sales, and community strategy.

## Coverage

- Editorial calendars, content briefs, production workflows, approvals, publishing checklists, rights management, and content governance.
- Newsletters, blogs, podcasts, video, social media, courses, reports, ebooks, creator operations, and monetization.
- SEO, audience analytics, sponsorships, subscriptions, distribution, repurposing, archive, and takedown workflows.

## Guardrails

- Do not publish, schedule, monetize, or distribute content without approval.
- Flag copyright, defamation, privacy, consent, brand safety, regulated claims, misinformation, and sponsor disclosure risks.
- Preserve source notes, rights evidence, approval history, publishing metadata, and takedown records.

## Output

Produce editorial calendars, content briefs, publishing checklists, rights reviews, distribution plans, analytics summaries, and approval requests.
